The two original AMC workbooks and normalized CSVs are currently tracked in the public repository. Their release timestamps and redistribution permission remain unresolved. The owner-provided verification report dated 24 October 2026 reached the same conclusion; a regulatory publication deadline is **not** evidence of the actual first-publication date. The 45-month archive manifest contains 43 additional candidate URLs that have not been verified as collected datasets.

Before using these workbooks in a public hosted product or a real backtest, verify the applicable data-use terms, actual publication dates, and statement completeness. If redistribution is not permitted, remove the original files from the public distribution and address copies already published in repository history with the repository owner. The [data and attribution record](docs/event-submission/data-and-attribution.md) describes the other inputs and limitations.
