# Data, attribution, and licensing record

## Data in the current repository

| Material | Provenance | Use and limit |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Synthetic holdings and market observations | Deterministically generated by `backend/app/demo.py`; companies and funds are fictional | Local and hosted UI/pipeline demonstration only; no real-world accuracy claim |
| Supplied ABSL Flexi Cap August and September workbooks and CSVs | Files under `data/absl_flexi_cap/`; both CSVs match the corresponding AMC workbooks | Two browsable snapshots; exact publication dates remain unverified, so neither is eligible for model labels. See [review](../absl-data-review.md). |
| Example CSV templates | `frontend/public/templates/`; example fields and rows | Schema illustration, not an official disclosure |
| User-imported holdings/market CSVs | Supplied by the operator, with source URL and report/publication dates recorded | Import does not independently verify the URL, source license, or permission |
| Company events | User-supplied source URL, publication time, and excerpt | Optional extraction must match identifiers/provenance and source text |

The [data-source register](../../DATA_SOURCES.md) records the known AMC URLs, hashes, dates, and rights status. The August and September AMC archives in the [acquisition manifest](../absl-data-acquisition.md) downloaded successfully with GET on 24 October 2026; HEAD had returned 404 earlier, so HEAD is not a reliable availability check for these files. Downloaded archive logs and NSE data live under ignored `data/absl_flexi_cap/downloads/`. No NSE/BSE market-feed or news dataset is bundled in versioned data. The owner-provided verification report did not establish the workbooks' first-publication dates or permission to republish the originals and screenshots. The original workbooks and normalized CSVs are already tracked in the public repository. A public page is not automatically an open data license.

## Limits to state in every review

The candidate target is a future **disclosed position**, not a trade. The ranker uses uncalibrated scores. User-supplied completeness and dates affect labels. Synthetic evaluation establishes software behavior only. Real predictive performance, customer value, and data rights still require verification.
